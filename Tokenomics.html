Token Utility

What You Can Do With the Token

The Cain token powers the entire platform — from governance to fee discounts, staking rewards to exclusive access.

01
Fee Income Rewards
Token holders receive a percentage of all protocol fee income — making long-term holders true stakeholders in Cain's revenue.
02
Swap Fee Discounts
Hold Cain tokens to unlock reduced spread on all swaps routed through the Cain aggregator — the more you hold, the less you pay.
03
Vault Access Tiers
Exclusive access to higher-tier vaults and strategies. Premium tiers unlocked at token thresholds — better APY for loyal participants.
04
Governance & DAO Voting
Participate in decentralized anonymous voting — vote on vault parameters, fee structures, presale allocation, and ecosystem grants. Requires at least one NFT or an active LP position to participate.
05
Cross-Platform Tipping
Tip CAIN tokens to other users on-site, on Discord, and on Reddit. Deposit and withdraw tokens via bots on both platforms — a prime utility tool for fostering community engagement.
06
Staking Returns
Stake CAIN tokens for 8–20% annual returns depending on lock duration. Flexible staking earns the base rate; longer locks earn higher yields.
07
Copy Trading
Follow and copy trade desired on-chain addresses. Token holders get priority access to the copy trading feature and reduced performance fees.
08
API Access
Free developer API access for token holders — enabling builders to integrate Cain's routing engine, analytics, and vault data into their own products.

Situation Room

Algorithm-Assisted Analysis
Situation Room
A deeply detailed dashboard that aggregates live news, commodity prices (oil, gold, metals), geopolitical developments (e.g. Strait of Hormuz tensions), macroeconomic indicators, and crypto-specific signals into a single view. The algorithm surfaces correlations and patterns — for example, how rising oil prices historically affect Solana or when S&P 500 dips create crypto buying opportunities. It's not a trading bot. It's a tool to help you form your own conclusions faster, backed by real data instead of headlines. Think of it as your personal research terminal for deciding whether to buy the S&P 500 or Solana today.
