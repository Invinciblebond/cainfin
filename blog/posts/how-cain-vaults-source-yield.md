A reasonable first question about any yield product is simple: *where does the yield actually come from?* If you can't answer that, you don't understand the risk. Here's how Cain vaults generate returns.

## Liquidity provision

The core engine is **LP yield**. The protocol routes capital into Solana's deepest liquidity pools and earns trading fees from the volume flowing through them. Deeper, more active markets generate more fees per dollar deposited.

## Real-world assets

A portion of capital is allocated to **real-world assets** backed by large institutions. These provide a yield floor that is largely uncorrelated with crypto market swings, which smooths the overall return profile.

## What we avoid

Just as important is what we *don't* do:

1. We don't chase unsustainable emissions that evaporate once incentives end.
2. We don't take on leverage the vault can't unwind cleanly.
3. We don't hide the source of yield behind abstractions.

The result is a return that's lower-drama by design — built for people playing the long game rather than the next farm.
