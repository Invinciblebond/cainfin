Welcome to the Cain Finance blog. This is where we'll share **protocol updates**, deep dives on the strategies behind each vault, and the reasoning that shapes how we route capital across Solana and real-world assets.

## Why a blog

Most of what matters in a yield protocol happens beneath the surface — rebalancing logic, risk parameters, where the yield actually comes from. Twitter threads disappear and Discord scrolls away. This is the durable record.

## What to expect

We'll publish on a few recurring themes:

- **Protocol updates** — new vaults, parameter changes, and roadmap progress.
- **Strategy breakdowns** — how a given vault sources yield and what the risks are.
- **Research** — the market structure and on-chain data behind our decisions.

> Capital should compound quietly. So should understanding.

You can subscribe to updates by following us on [X](https://x.com/CainFinance) or joining the [Discord](https://discord.gg/k8STqQgFuM). More soon.
