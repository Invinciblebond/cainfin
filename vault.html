About

The S&P500 LP vault accepts USDC deposits on Solana and zaps 100% of capital into the Bitget-backed byreal CLMM SPYx-USDC pool at a user-selected LP width.

Returns come from trading fees earned on the SPYx-USDC pair and are reflected directly in the S&P500 LP token price rather than distributed as yield — providing passive, compounding exposure to concentrated liquidity returns entirely on-chain. Upon redemption, USDC is returned with no fees.

The vault is designed for users who want LP yield without manually managing tick ranges, rebalancing, or fee collection.

Strategy

The S&P500 LP vault provides on-chain, passive access to concentrated-liquidity fee income from the SPYx-USDC trading pair on Solana. Capital is zapped into a byreal CLMM position at the user's chosen LP width, with fees auto-compounded back into the position.


Designed for users who want LP returns without managing positions manually. The pool is hosted on Bitget's byreal.io CLMM DEX; the S&P500 LP pool trades SPYx against USDC.

Risk FAQ

Is the 13% APY real?
Yes — this is how concentrated liquidity pools work. The APR is derived from actual trading fees collected by the SPYx-USDC pool on byreal CLMM, annualised from the last 30 days of fee data. Fees vary with market volatility and trading volume; past performance does not guarantee future returns.
What fees does Cain charge?
The $0.20 flat fee per deposit and withdrawal is the only fee Cain takes.
What happens if my LP range goes out of bounds?
If the SPYx price moves outside your selected LP width, your position stops earning fees until the price returns to range. You will receive an email notification when your position goes out of range. Auto-adjustment will soon be available as a toggle — for now, you can manually rebalance or wait for price to re-enter your range.
